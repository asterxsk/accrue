## Review 2 — independent reviewer verdict

GOOD, with six defects. Fixed in the source:

1. Insights comparison bars raised to a visible neutral step: new `bar` token, `#3A3A3A` dark / `#DCDCDC` light.
   Previously they sat at roughly 1.3:1 and read as skeletons.
2. Routine segments now show two completed plus the current step, matching "Step 3 of 4".
3. Light-mode outlines fixed: new `line-strong` token `#BFBFBF` on unchecked circles, stepper rings, back
   buttons, unselected radios, empty dots, and ghost buttons. Dark value `#4D4D4D`.
4. New habit: the name field no longer uses the same full-strength border as the selected option; focus reads
   from the caret, selection from the accent border.
5. "Next up" discs take `line-strong`, glyphs take `ink`, so both are legible in dark.
6. The trend marker is now a filled triangle instead of a text-like caret.

Still open from this audit: #2 sample-data labelling, #3 loading and error states, #4 focus-ring spec, #5 radius
consolidation.

## Review 3 — three-lens pass over the 18 renders

Run as three parallel reviewers (navigation, craft, theme parity) after the three-tab restructure. 25 findings;
these were applied:

Navigation. Settings page retitled from "You" to "Settings". New habit lost its modal "Cancel" header and took
the shared 44pt circular back button, so all five pushed screens now dismiss the same way. Routine run content
was a second, contradictory definition of Morning; it now shows step 3 as Journal, 3 min, after stretch, with
Cold shower as next up, matching screen 8.

Contrast, fixed at the token level so every screen moves together:

- `border` dark raised `#191919` to `#262626`: progress tracks and unfilled routine segments were 1.13:1 and read
  as missing. This also darkens hairlines, which is the intended trade.
- `surface` dark raised `#111111` to `#141414` and `border` carries the card edge, so a dark card separates from
  the page as clearly as a light one does.
- `line-strong` raised to `#52525b` dark / `#b8b8c0` light: hollow rings (upcoming steps, undrunk glasses,
  unselected radios) were 1.5:1 and vanished.
- `bar` raised to `#4a4a52` dark / `#d4d4d8` light: six of seven Insights bars were near-invisible.
- `accent` dark nudged `#346bf1` to `#2f63e8`, taking small white glyphs on the fill from 4.65:1 to about 5.1:1.

Craft. Routine steps now share the card's text column (disc centre 36, label x68) instead of sitting 14pt left of
the routine name. Routine cards carry a chevron so the path to the run screen is visible. The Routines header
action moved to the same slot as the profile avatar. The timer's unfilled track now matches the arc's 7pt stroke
at the arc's radius instead of sitting outside it as a 2pt hairline. The timer digits were 5.5pt below the ring
centre. The Insights trend triangle sat 5.5px low on the numerals. The counter week strip was flush left with a
48pt right gutter; it now distributes across the content column. Deep work's chevron shares the Water stepper's
right edge.

Deliberately kept, with reasons:

- Dark text accent `#7aa2ff` against fill `#346bf1` is two blues on purpose: T3's blue fails as small text on
  black, so text and icons take the lighter tone and fills keep the intended hue. Light mode has one blue because
  it can.
- "Browse routine ideas" stays on the empty state. With no habits, a second entry into Routines is a recovery
  path, not a duplicate tab.
- Today keeps its two routine cards. They are the glance; the tab is the management surface.
- Today's last card clears the floating pill by 10pt. A 100pt scroll inset belongs in a coded implementation, not
  in a static frame.

Follow-up after owner review: the step numerals were still set in a 22pt box anchored at the disc's old x, so each
digit sat 5pt left of its circle. Numerals are now inside the disc's own box. The same pass caught the ghost
button's plus icon, which had been dragged into the header by an over-broad selector; it is back at the button's
left edge, and the header plus is a separate layer centred in its 40pt button.
